PEOPLE OF THE TIME
Here is a list of well-known Romans and Greeks who were alive or otherwise very popular in the first century BCE:

### Romans

1. **Julius Caesar (100-44 BCE)**
   - Renowned military general, statesman, and key figure in the events leading to the demise of the Roman Republic and the rise of the Roman Empire.
   - Best known for his conquest of Gaul and his role in the Roman civil wars.
   - Famous works include "Commentarii de Bello Gallico" (Commentaries on the Gallic War).

2. **Cicero (106-43 BCE)**
   - Roman orator, lawyer, and statesman.
   - Known for his speeches against Catiline and his extensive correspondence which provides insight into the political life of the era.
   - Major works include "De Re Publica" and "De Legibus".

3. **Pompey the Great (106-48 BCE)**
   - Military and political leader of the late Roman Republic.
   - Part of the First Triumvirate with Julius Caesar and Crassus.
   - Known for his campaigns in the Eastern Mediterranean and against the pirates.

4. **Marcus Licinius Crassus (115-53 BCE)**
   - Wealthiest man in Roman history, known for his role in the First Triumvirate.
   - Noted for his defeat of the Spartacus-led slave rebellion.
   - Died in the Parthian campaign at the Battle of Carrhae.

5. **Mark Antony (83-30 BCE)**
   - Roman politician and general, ally of Julius Caesar, and member of the Second Triumvirate.
   - Known for his relationship with Cleopatra VII of Egypt.
   - Played a critical role in the transformation of the Roman Republic into the Roman Empire.

6. **Octavian/Augustus (63 BCE - 14 CE)**
   - Founder of the Roman Empire and its first Emperor.
   - Defeated Mark Antony and Cleopatra at the Battle of Actium.
   - Ushered in the Pax Romana, a period of relative peace and stability across the Empire.

7. **Gaius Marius (157-86 BCE)**
   - Roman general and statesman.
   - Known for his military reforms and multiple consulships.
   - Played a significant role in the Social War and the civil war against Sulla.

### Greeks

1. **Cleopatra VII (69-30 BCE)**
   - The last active ruler of the Ptolemaic Kingdom of Egypt.
   - Known for her relationships with Julius Caesar and Mark Antony.
   - Played a significant political role in the Roman civil wars.

2. **Strabo (64/63 BCE - 24 CE)**
   - Greek geographer, philosopher, and historian.
   - Best known for his work "Geographica," an encyclopedia of geographical knowledge.

3. **Parmenion (c. 400-330 BCE)**
   - Macedonian general and father of Philotas.
   - Served under Philip II and Alexander the Great.
   - Played a key role in several major battles, including the Battle of Gaugamela.

4. **Diodorus Siculus (c. 90-30 BCE)**
   - Greek historian.
   - Known for his work "Bibliotheca historica," a universal history of the world from mythological times to his own era.

5. **Zeno of Sidon (c. 150-75 BCE)**
   - Prominent Epicurean philosopher.
   - Known for his teachings and influence on Epicureanism during the first century BCE.

6. **Nicander of Colophon (c. 2nd century BCE)**
   - Greek poet and physician.
   - Known for his didactic poems "Theriaca" and "Alexipharmaca," which focus on venomous animals and antidotes.

These individuals were influential in various fields such as politics, military, philosophy, and literature, shaping the cultural and historical landscape of their time.
