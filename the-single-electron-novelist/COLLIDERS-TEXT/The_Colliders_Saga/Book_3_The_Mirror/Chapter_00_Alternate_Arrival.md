### Arrival in Rome

After what felt like an eternity, the coastline of Italy finally came into view. The trireme sailed up the Tiber River, the familiar sights of Rome growing closer with each passing hour. Lucius, exhausted but resolute, remained at his post until the ship docked at the bustling port of Ostia.

He oversaw the careful unloading of the crate, ensuring it was transported safely to the Ludus Scientiae. As he escorted the precious cargo through the streets of Rome, he felt a sense of accomplishment and relief. The journey had been long and arduous, but he had fulfilled his duty.

Lucius delivered the crate to Marcus Tullius Varro, who greeted him with a knowing smile. “Well done, Lucius. You’ve done more than you realize. Now, let us see what secrets this device holds.”

As Varro began to unseal the crate, Lucius couldn’t help but feel a surge of anticipation. The journey was over, but the adventure—and the learning—had just begun.
