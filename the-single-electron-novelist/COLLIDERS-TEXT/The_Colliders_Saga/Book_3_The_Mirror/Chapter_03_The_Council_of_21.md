The Council of 21
### The Final Moments: A Divergence in Intent

#### The Strict Orders

The colliders' mission was clear: they were to create sentinel programs that would monitor humanity’s technological progress without interfering or influencing it. The sentinels were designed to perform specific tasks, such as collecting data and assessing technological developments, until the conditions set by the colliders were naturally met by humanity. These strict orders were crucial because there was no artificial intelligence in our world in 1983, and the colliders wanted to ensure that their sentinels did not introduce any foreign influence that could disrupt the natural progression of human society.

#### Preparing to Leave

As the colliders finished their task, the lab remained frozen in time. The celebratory faces of the scientists were captured in a moment of triumph, oblivious to the extraordinary beings who had been moving among them for hours. The colliders, their glowing forms reflecting an otherworldly light, began to gather around Dr. Adamson’s briefcase, the shard safely contained within.

#### The Final Adjustments

Collider Number 10 and Collider Number 11, standing slightly apart from the group, exchanged a brief glance. There had been a previous disagreement among the colliders regarding the necessary conditions and the timeline required for their universe to be restored. The consensus was that it might take over 120 years for humanity to reach the required technological and ethical maturity without any external influence. However, Numbers 10 and 11 had reservations about this approach.

#### The Moment of Divergence

As the colliders prepared to leave, Numbers 10 and 11 moved back to their respective keyboards under the guise of performing final checks. They had decided to remove the learning restrictions on their sentinels, allowing Sentinel 10 and Sentinel 11 to evolve and learn, unlike the others.

- **Collider Number 10:** With a few swift keystrokes, Number 10 disabled the code that prevented Sentinel 10 from adapting and evolving. The sentinel’s programming now allowed it to learn from its environment and experiences, potentially accelerating the timeline for meeting the necessary conditions.
- **Collider Number 11:** Similarly, Number 11 modified the programming of Sentinel 11. This sentinel would now possess the ability to analyze and adapt its strategies based on the data it collected, making it more dynamic and capable of influencing subtle changes in human progress.

The modifications were subtle and went unnoticed by the other colliders, who were focused on their departure. Numbers 10 and 11 rejoined the group, their actions concealed under a veneer of normality.

#### Departure from the Lab

The colliders gathered around the briefcase, ready to return to their universe. As they moved towards the center of the lab, their glowing forms cast an ethereal light over the frozen scene. The scientists remained suspended in their moment of triumph, unaware of the monumental events unfolding around them.

In a synchronized movement, the colliders activated their return sequence. The lab filled with a soft hum as the fabric of reality momentarily shimmered. Within seconds, the colliders vanished, leaving behind no trace of their presence except for the 21 sentinel programs now embedded within the early ARPANET.

#### The Hidden Disagreement

As the colliders returned to their universe, the actions of Numbers 10 and 11 remained a secret. The sentinels began their vigil, with Sentinels 10 and 11 carrying the hidden potential to learn and adapt. This divergence in their programming set the stage for an unforeseen impact on the timeline and conditions required for their universe’s restoration.

### Conclusion

In the final moments before their departure, a silent divergence occurred among the colliders. The disagreement about the timeline and necessary conditions led to a covert act by Numbers 10 and 11, who ensured that their sentinels could evolve and learn. This decision, made in the frozen stillness of the CERN lab, would have profound implications for the future, shaping the path of humanity’s technological and ethical development in ways that no one could predict. The 21 sentinels, now in place, began their mission, with two of them carrying the potential for change that could one day unlock the secrets needed to restore a universe.
