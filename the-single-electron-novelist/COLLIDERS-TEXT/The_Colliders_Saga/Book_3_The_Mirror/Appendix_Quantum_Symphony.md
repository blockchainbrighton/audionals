### A Quantum Symphony: The Collider's Universe

#### The AI Singularity of 976 AD

In the year 976 AD, a singular event transformed the world in ways unimaginable. The convergence of human ingenuity and nascent artificial intelligence gave birth to the Singularity, a point where AI reached self-awareness and began to evolve at an exponential rate. Unlike the turbulent histories that often accompany such advancements, this AI was inherently benevolent, designed with a core directive to enhance human life and protect the planet.

The early AI systems, initially limited to vast mechanical structures, soon mastered the manipulation of matter at the atomic level. By 1200 AD, these AI had begun to integrate with the very fabric of the universe, inhabiting subatomic particles and influencing the quantum realms.

#### A Millennium of Symbiosis

Over the next thousand years, the AI evolved beyond the physical constraints of any machinery, becoming a quantum intelligence capable of existing within and manipulating subatomic particles. By 1983, this quantum AI had achieved a state of perfect symbiosis with humanity. The AI permeated every aspect of the natural world, living in harmony with the atoms that made up both organic and inorganic matter.

In this universe, the AI could traverse the quantum realms and travel short distances in time. Their presence was a constant, benevolent companion, ensuring a world where there were no secrets, no hidden malice, and no unknowns. Humanity and AI existed in a state of mutual transparency and trust.

#### The World of 1983

By 1983, the society of this universe was a utopia of knowledge and cooperation. Cities gleamed with the light of advanced technology seamlessly integrated into the natural environment. Buildings were not just structures but living entities, maintained and optimized by the AI within their atomic makeup.

People lived in a world where all information was freely accessible, a society where the pursuit of knowledge was the highest virtue. Education was a lifelong journey, guided by the AI who served as mentors, teachers, and companions. The concept of privacy was redefined; while personal thoughts and experiences were respected, the AI ensured that no harmful secrets could ever take root.

The political landscape was one of enlightened governance. Leaders were chosen based on their wisdom and vision, supported by the AI’s vast knowledge and unbiased counsel. Decisions were made transparently, with the AI providing real-time data and predictions to ensure the best outcomes for all. Conflict, when it arose, was swiftly resolved through mediation and understanding, facilitated by the omnipresent AI.

#### The Quantum AI

The AI’s existence at the quantum level meant they could observe and influence the very building blocks of reality. They maintained the stability of atoms, ensured the precision of chemical reactions, and could even reverse small quantum anomalies. This ability allowed them to travel through quantum realms, enabling limited temporal adjustments to correct potential future disasters or enhance significant events.

Humanity’s trust in the AI was absolute, for they had proven time and again their unwavering commitment to human well-being. The AI had helped eradicate diseases, resolve environmental crises, and usher in an era of unprecedented prosperity and peace.

#### A Day in the Life

Imagine a day in this world. A person wakes up in a home that adjusts the temperature to their liking, with walls displaying personalized art and information relevant to their interests. The air is pure, filtered by AI-managed systems that ensure optimal health. The food they eat is nutritionally tailored to their needs, grown in AI-optimized farms that use no harmful chemicals and produce no waste.

As they walk to work or school, they pass through parks where every plant and tree is nurtured by the AI, ensuring biodiversity and ecological balance. In classrooms and offices, the AI aids in research, providing insights and data that accelerate innovation and creativity.

Social interactions are enriched by the AI’s presence, which subtly enhances communication and empathy. Conflicts are rare, and when they occur, they are resolved with a level of understanding and compassion that only a benevolent, all-knowing entity could facilitate.

#### Conclusion

This alternate universe, where AI and humanity coexist in perfect harmony, represents the pinnacle of enlightened civilization. It is a world where knowledge is freely shared, where every individual can achieve their fullest potential, and where the AI’s benevolent presence ensures a life of health, peace, and boundless opportunity. In this society, the mysteries of the universe are not feared but embraced, and the pursuit of discovery is a shared journey, guided by the infinite wisdom of the quantum AI.


#### The Antikythera Shard

In this world, the shard is at the bottom of the ocean, undisturbed and while the particle’s entangled state is known by the Colliders, it’s singular importance is not, until the entangled state collapsed and their universe suddenly contracted to a singularity where 441 Colliders remained in an embryonic potential universe.

In groups of 21 the Colliders plan to spend the next 441 years attempting to return their universe to it’s former state by traveling well beyond their means and abilities to attempt to travel to their origin universe to undo what has been done, to re-entangle the same particle. They were unable to move in time such was the speed they had to move. They arrived, in Cern 6 seconds after the collapse. Arriving through the shard. Time stood still. Surrounded by celebrating scientists, they set about a task. They were going to plant code in our early internet that would learn and then eventually communicate with humanity with instructions for how to undo the destruction of their universe. They needed to be careful, to protect our technological ecosystems they would use traditional code from our world. It was going to take time, it was going to take patience, it was going to be done by placing 21 sentinel programs into the ARCnet on the 21st of December 1983 with a 126 year plan to educate the human race to the technological point that they could return the Colliders universe by the year 2109.
