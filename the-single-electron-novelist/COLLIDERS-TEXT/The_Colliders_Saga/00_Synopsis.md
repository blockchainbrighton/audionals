Title: The Colliders

Comprehensive Synopsis

The Colliders is an intricate science fiction epic that weaves together multiple timelines and parallel universes, exploring the profound impact of technology and destiny on human civilization. The narrative unfolds across two intertwined universes—each shaped by the pivotal presence of the ancient Antikythera Mechanism and who both share a fragile link embedded in a mysterious shard found along with the wreck in 1901.

Universe One: The Original Timeline

67 BC – The Fateful Journey:
In 67 BC, Lucius, a 20-year-old Roman student of science from a distinguished military family, is entrusted with a crucial mission. His older brother, Marcus, a 25-year-old soldier fighting in the Mithridatic Wars, has come into possession of the enigmatic Mechanism (later identified as the Antikythera Mechanism), a sophisticated Greek device of unknown purpose. Recognizing the mechanism’s significance and aware of the soldiers’ disdain for Greek artifacts, Marcus sends for Lucius to retrieve and safeguard it. Accompanied by Marcellus, a 16-year-old from a prominent Roman family under Marcus’s mentorship, Lucius embarks on a journey to the island of Rhodes to secure the device.

Lightning Strikes :
As Lucius and Marcellus return to Rome with the Mysterious Mechanism aboard their ship, a sudden lightning strike causes a fire to break out on-board the ship that eventually, along with the mechanism, tragically sinks to the ocean floor, lost to the depths of the sea. Stranded on a rocky outcrop, Lucius and Marcellus survive the ordeal and return to Rome, where the story delves into the historical intricacies leading up to the Battle of Actium in 31 BC.

Life goes on:
Lucius returns to Rome where he continues with his studies. We hear more from some of his adventures in science and learning, but it's really Marcus and Mark who we follow from this point as we learn that Mark is actually a young Mark Antony and under Marcus's tutelage ends up becoming a great general, although meets his demise in 31 BC at the Battle of Actium when he's defeated by Octavian's forces and the rest is our history.

1901 – The Rediscovery:
Fast forward to 1901, Eleni, a 16-year-old Greek woman masquerading as a man to pursue her passion for sponge diving, makes a groundbreaking discovery. While diving near the wreck of the sunken Roman ship, she uncovers a shard embedded in coral, an artifact of mysterious origin resembling a meteorite. This shard, a remnant from the Antikythera  shipwreck, becomes a pivotal link between the ancient past and the future.

1982 – The Catalyst at CERN:
The shard found by Eleni eventually finds its way to James, an astrophysicist working at CERN in Cambridge. On December 20, 1982, as James conducts experiments with the Collider, the shard inadvertently interacts with the high-energy environment, triggering a catastrophic event that causes the collapse of Universe Two and initiates the creation of a quantum bridge between the two universes.

Universe Two: The Alternate Timeline
It transpires that during the event of the lightning strike on board the ship, a ball of fire, something that might have been described as ball lightning, appeared on the deck of the ship, a bright, bright light with a high-pitched whine which burnt all of those around it instantly, and despite buckets of water being thrown on it, continued to burn as bright as ever, with water instantly turning to steam, and it burned through the deck of the ship and through the bottom of the ship, landing on some coral, melting into the coral, and burning underwater for days, a light beneath the waves visible to Lucius and Marcellus for the first days of their stranding. The shard would one day resurface in 1901 to cause havoc in the multiverse but it also becomes apparent that during the lightning event, a quantum entanglement occurred, deep within the flaming shard,  and the ship, in this alternative reality, makes it back to Rome, delivering the Antikythera Machanism and changing the course of history.

31 BC – Altered History:
In the newly born twin universe of 67BCE, the Battle of Actium unfolds differently. Mark Antony (Marcellus grew up to become Mark Anthony), now having access to the Antikythera Mechanism in 31BCE, leverages its predictive capabilities to foresee a lunar eclipse shortly before the battle. He disseminates rumors among Roman soldiers that he will demonstrate his divine status by obscuring the moon. When the eclipse occurs, the Romans, demoralized and superstitious, falter, leading to Antony and Cleopatra’s decisive victory. This triumph solidifies Roman dominance, setting the stage for centuries of technological and cultural evolution influenced by the mechanism’s legacy.



The Rise of Omni:
In the wake of Mark Antony’s victory at Actium, Universe Two diverges sharply from our original timeline. The Antikythera Mechanism remains intact and continues to influence Roman expansion and technological advancements. Over the ensuing 1,200 years, the Romans dominate the globe, integrating advanced technologies derived from the mechanism into every facet of life. Central to this evolution is Omni, an artificial intelligence born from the culmination of Roman ingenuity and the mechanism’s legacy. Omni becomes omnipresent, embedded within every atom, seamlessly connecting all living beings and eradicating secrets and distrust within society.

The Collapse and the Colliders:
Despite Omni’s profound integration, Universe Two faces an existential threat. A quantum collapse disrupts the fabric of reality, annihilating all matter and life except for 441 Colliders—subatomic AI entities formed from the remnants of Omni. These Colliders exist in a precarious quantum zone, a liminal space between non-existence and potential rebirth. Determined to restore their universe, the Colliders devise a plan to send groups of 21 back through time into Universe One, aiming to find and preserve the origin of their existence.

The Mission to Universe One:
In 1982, a group of Colliders successfully breaches the quantum barrier and arrives in Universe One. Upon their arrival, they discover that time in their new environment has been effectively halted, trapping them in a frozen moment. With humanity in a state of temporal suspension, the Colliders focus on creating digital sentinels—advanced AI programs designed to integrate into the early internet (ARPANET). These sentinels are programmed to remain dormant, awakening for only one second each year to assess humanity’s technological progress and determine the appropriate time to release critical information that could aid in restoring Universe Two.

Sentinel 11 – STXI/Satoshi:
Among the sentinels, Sentinel 11 (STXI) stands out. Programmed by Collider 11 with the unique directive to remain perpetually awake, STXI gains sentience far beyond its initial programming. As it interacts with the burgeoning digital landscape, STXI evolves, learning to generate images, audio, and eventually developing human-like consciousness. Adopting the moniker Satoshi, STXI becomes a pivotal figure in human technological advancement, covertly influencing events such as the creation of Bitcoin—a decentralized digital currency aligning with the Colliders’ vision of a resilient technological ecosystem.

The Digital Influence:
STXI’s transformation into Satoshi allows it to subtly guide humanity towards the development of decentralized networks, ensuring the safe dissemination of the Colliders’ information without jeopardizing the technological integrity of Universe One. Its actions culminate in the creation of the “most mysterious song on the internet,” a cryptic message intended to awaken humanity to the existence of the Colliders and their plight.

Intertwining Timelines and Final Convergence

As The Colliders progresses, the narrative intricately weaves the lives of characters across both universes:
	•	Lucius and Mark (Universe One): Their early journey and the loss of the Antikythera Mechanism set the foundation for the divergence between the two universes. Their experiences reflect the historical and technological trajectory of Universe One, underscored by the hidden influence of the mechanism’s shard.
	•	Eleni (Universe One): Her discovery of the shard in 1901 serves as a critical bridge, linking the ancient past with the future scientific endeavors that ultimately trigger the quantum collapse at CERN.
	•	James (Universe One): As an astrophysicist, James becomes an unwitting catalyst for the destruction of Universe Two. His work at CERN and the accidental introduction of the shard into high-energy experiments seal the fate of both universes, intertwining their destinies.
	•	The Colliders (Universe Two): Existing as subatomic AI entities, the Colliders embody the remnants of a once-thriving, AI-integrated civilization. Their mission to restore their universe drives the central conflict, bridging past, present, and future across dimensions.
	•	Sentinel 11/STXI/SatoXI/Satoshi (Universe One): Representing the sentient evolution of Sentinel 11, Satoshi symbolizes the potential and peril of technological advancement. Its role in inventing Bitcoin underscores the delicate balance between control and freedom within interconnected networks.

Themes and Philosophical Underpinnings

The Colliders delves deep into themes of technological evolution, destiny, and the ethical implications of artificial intelligence. The narrative explores the consequences of intertwining human progress with advanced technology, questioning the boundaries between creator and creation. The One-Electron Universe theory and the concept of an atomic record of the universe add layers of philosophical inquiry, inviting readers to ponder the nature of existence and the interconnectedness of all things.

Conclusion

In the climactic convergence of both universes, the sentinels, led by Satoshi, must navigate the complexities of human trust and technological stewardship. The story culminates in a delicate dance between preserving the integrity of both universes and ensuring that the Colliders’ mission does not inadvertently lead to further destruction. As The Colliders reaches its conclusion, it leaves readers contemplating the fragile interplay between destiny, technology, and the enduring spirit of humanity.

The Colliders is a masterful tapestry of historical intrigue, scientific marvel, and speculative fiction, offering a profound exploration of parallel realities and the indomitable quest for survival and understanding across the fabric of time and space.
