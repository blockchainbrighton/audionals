The Storm Near Antikythera
As the trireme sailed near the island of Antikythera, a sudden and violent storm descended upon them. The sky darkened, and the wind howled, whipping the sea into a frenzy. The ship was tossed about by the towering waves, and the crew struggled to maintain control. Lucius clung to the mast, his eyes wide with fear and wonder at the raw power of the storm.

The Lightning Strike
Without warning, a blinding flash of lightning struck the mast of the ship. The intense heat and energy coursed through the wood, and part of the lightning arced towards the crate lashed to the mast. There was a deafening crack, and the crate exploded with a burst of molten material that sprayed across the deck. Lucius watched in horror as the fiery substance ignited everything it touched.

The Unquenchable Fire
The crew desperately tried to extinguish the flames, but to their astonishment, the molten material refused to go out. Buckets of seawater seemed to have no effect, as the fire burned even brighter, consuming the wooden deck and everything in its path. The flames quickly spread to the lower levels of the ship, setting them ablaze as well.
Lucius, ever curious despite the danger, noticed how the molten material burned with an intensity unlike anything he had ever seen. It melted through the deck, creating a gaping hole that extended to the lower levels. The fire continued to burn through the ship’s structure, unstoppable and relentless.

The Shipwreck
The flames eventually reached the bottom of the ship, burning through the hull. With a final, devastating blaze, the ship began to break apart and sink. The crew and passengers scrambled to save themselves, but the fiery material had done its damage. The ship was doomed.
Lucius, clutching a piece of the broken crate, managed to find a makeshift raft. As the trireme sank beneath the waves, he floated away, watching the fiery glow disappear into the depths of the ocean. The storm began to subside, leaving him adrift in the calm but dark sea.

Stranded on the Rocky Atoll
Hours later, Lucius found himself washed ashore on a rocky atoll. Exhausted and shaken, he dragged the remains of the crate onto the rocks and collapsed beside it. When he regained his strength, he inspected the crate, only to find that one side had been blown out. The urn and its mysterious contents were gone, lost to the deep ocean.
Lucius felt a pang of despair, knowing that the precious device and ingredient Varro had entrusted him with were gone. He sat on the rocky atoll, surrounded by the vast, empty sea, contemplating his next move. The adventure had taken a perilous turn, but Lucius’s determination remained unshaken. He knew he had to find a way back to Rome and report the tragic loss to his teacher.
As he stared out at the horizon, a resolve began to form within him. He would not give up. The journey was far from over, and he would find a way to continue his quest, no matter the obstacles.
