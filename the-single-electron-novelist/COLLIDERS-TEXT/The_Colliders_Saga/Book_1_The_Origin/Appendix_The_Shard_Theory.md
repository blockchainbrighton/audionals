### The Tale of The Shard: A Singular Quantum Connection

#### The Lightning Strike

In the seventh decade BCE, a trireme carrying a precious scientific device and an urn containing Monazite sailed into a fierce storm near the island of Antikythera. The ship's mast was struck by a bolt of lightning, igniting a nuclear reaction within the urn. This reaction caused molten material to burst forth, setting the ship ablaze and embedding an entangled particle into a shard of pottery.

#### The Divergence of Realities

At that precise moment, a rare quantum event occurred, creating a divergent universe. This divergence resulted in two parallel realities, each containing a shard of pottery with the entangled particle. In our universe, the shard fell into the ocean along with the rest of the shipwreck. In the twin universe, the shard and some of the molten material passed through the bottom of the boat, sinking alone to the ocean floor.
