Among the treasures they recovered was the enigmatic Antikythera mechanism and the shard of pottery. The shard, seemingly unremarkable, was cataloged and studied as part of the collection.

The shard's journey from the ocean floor to a museum display was a testament to human curiosity and the relentless pursuit of knowledge.
