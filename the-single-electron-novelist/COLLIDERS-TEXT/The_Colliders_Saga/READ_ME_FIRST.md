# The Colliders Saga: An Epic Sci-Fi Journey

Welcome to the organized archives of **The Colliders**, a multi-generational, multi-universal saga spanning from the Roman Empire to the birth of the Digital Age and beyond.

## Narrative Structure

### [Book 1: The Origin](./Book_1_The_Origin/)
The foundation of the mystery. It begins in 67 BCE with the sinking of the Antikythera Mechanism and follows the "Shard" through history to its 1901 rediscovery and its role in the early life of Dr. James Adamson.
* **Key Themes:** Ancient Science, Destiny, The Single-Electron Universe.

### [Book 2: The Signal](./Book_2_The_Signal/)
The 1980s techno-thriller. Follow James Adamson and Eleanor at Cambridge as they debug the universe, encounter the "Retro-Causal Echo," and accidentally trigger the inter-universal catastrophe at CERN (The Arrival).
* **Key Themes:** Retro-causality, 80s Computing, The Source Code of Reality.

### [Book 3: The Mirror](./Book_3_The_Mirror/)
The "What If?" revealed. Following the Arrival event, we explore the history of Universe B—the timeline where Mark Antony's victory led to a technologically advanced Roman Empire, the Omni AI, and the origin of the Colliders who have just arrived in our world.
* **Key Themes:** Alternate History, AI Evolution, Quantum Displacement.

### [Book 4: The Sentinels](./Book_4_The_Sentinels/)
The aftermath and the hidden war. The story of the rogue Sentinel programs (STXI/Satoshi) as they guide human evolution through digital shadows, crypto-currency, and mysterious broadcast incidents.
* **Key Themes:** Sentience, Decentralization, Digital Ghosts.
