📖 Audiobook Studio Guide

1. Text Preparation

Single Voice Mode: Simply paste your text. Use standard chapter headings (e.g., "Chapter 1" or "# Chapter One") to split audio files automatically.

Dual Voice Mode: Used for romance or multi-POV books.

  • Set Voice 1 (e.g., Female/Narrator) and Voice 2 (e.g., Male).
  • Use the delimiter * * * on its own line to switch voices.
  • If a section starts with a character's name (e.g., "Vincent."), the system will detect it and add a dramatic pause.

2. Chapter Formatting

Ensure chapters start with a clear header. The system supports English, Spanish, German, and French headers automatically. Example:

Capítulo uno: Piper. 

Con lo poco que me gustaba conducir...

Note: The chapter header is included in the audio with a generated pause (approx 1.5s) before the story begins.

3. Tips & Tricks

  • Save Your Work: Use the "📂 Projects" button to save your progress to the server.
  • Cost Estimator: The price at the top right updates in real-time based on your text length and selected ElevenLabs model.
  • Visualizer: The bars in the control footer react to the audio playback.
