# Audiobook Conversion Master Plan

## 1. Opportunity Snapshot
- Major publishers (PRH, HarperCollins, Hachette, S&S, Macmillan, Bloomsbury) are scaling from hundreds to thousands of titles per year yet still cite narrator scarcity and long studio queues (`Publishers Converting Their Book Catalogues into Audiobooks.docx`).
- Independents (Sourcebooks, Storm Publishing, Bookouture, Jacaranda, New Island, LeYa, Amar Chitra Katha, Muswell Press, Podium) rely on external partners like PRH Audio, Dreamscape, Saga Egmont, Bolinda for throughput (`Publishers Converting Their Book Catalogues into Audiobooks.docx`, `indy publishers 2`).
- Status quo economics: 75–80k word title ≈ 9–10 finished hours (FH). Human narration $250–400/FH, editing $50–125/FH, mastering/admin $25–50/FH → $3k–$7k cash cost + 4–6 weeks production + queue time. AI pilots (e.g., HarperCollins ElevenLabs) aim for $800–$1.5k/title but still need manual polish.
- Value proposition: hybrid AI + human QC pipeline delivering mastered files in ≤5 business days for ≤$2k/title, plus optional multilingual/localized outputs and marketing-ready deliverables.

## 2. Research Flywheel (repeatable inputs)
1. **Platform Sweeps (weekly):** Search Audible, Spotify, Apple Books, Storytel for publisher/imprint pages to spot catalogue spikes (method already highlighted in both research docs).
2. **Press & Deal Monitoring (bi-weekly):** Track Publishers Weekly, The Bookseller, Publishing Perspectives, APA reports, Frankfurt/LBF coverage using saved queries “publisher + audiobook + year” or “audio imprint/deal”.
3. **Distributor Watchlist (monthly):** Subscribe to Saga Egmont, Bookwire, RBmedia/W.F. Howes, Findaway, Dreamscape, Bolinda updates—new partner mentions = hot leads.
4. **Hiring Signal Scrape (monthly):** Monitor LinkedIn/jobs for “audio rights manager,” “audio producer,” “head of audio” to catch publishers investing ahead of announcements.
5. **Regional Keyword Rotation (monthly):** Search local terms (“audiolibro editorial,” “livro áudio editora,” “Hörbuch Verlag”) to surface non‑English publishers launching audio programs.
6. **Rights/Deal Feeds (ongoing):** Review rights newsletters for “world audio rights” clauses; log publisher + contact + title.

Track outputs in Airtable/Notion with columns: publisher, signal source, annual audio volume estimate, current partner, contact info, outreach status, next step.

## 6. Execution Checklist
1. **Set up tooling:** Notion/Airtable tracker, templated outreach emails, secure file transfer, QA checklists, narrator roster, localization partners.
2. **Gather assets:** Pilot offer PDF, process overview, sample clips, credentials.
3. **Research cadence:** Schedule recurring reminders for platform sweeps, press scans, distributor feeds, job-signal scrapes, and regional keyword searches.
4. **Outreach cadence:** Send at least two targeted emails per segment (enterprise, indie, aggregator) each week; log replies, schedule discovery calls.
5. **Pilot readiness:** Prepare automation scripts (text-to-speech presets, mastering chains) + human QC SOPs to guarantee 5-day delivery for pilot manuscripts.
6. **Post-pilot funnel:** Upon positive feedback, present multi-title proposal (volume pricing + SLA) and optional localization add-ons.
7. **Marketing collateral:** Collect testimonials/case studies after first paid projects to strengthen future outreach.
8. **Quarterly review:** Update prospect list using new signals from research flywheel; retire unresponsive leads after 3 touches and replace with fresh ones.

Delivering consistent research + rapid pilots turns this plan into a standing pipeline of publishers with the budget and urgency to outsource audiobook production.
